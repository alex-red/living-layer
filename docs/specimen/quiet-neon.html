00 · Principles

Apple sets the rules. The city sets the mood.

The one warm light

Amber is the lamp's own 2700 K. If something glows warm, a real light is involved.

Colour means a source

Cyan is what the model saw. Magenta is what you decided. Amber is what happened in the room.

Never grade the evidence

Grain and scanlines live on the frame. The pixels a decision used stay true colour.

Defer to the scene

The camera frame is the hero. Everything around it is glass and type.

Telemetry whispers

Mono labels like OBS-0142 add flavour. Instructions stay plain sentences.

Calm by default

Nothing flickers on its own. Motion happens because something changed.

05 · Motion

Moves only when something changed

Ease-out for most things, a small spring for the things you touch. With reduced motion, everything becomes a short fade.

Ease-out · 400ms vs spring · segmented control, capsule
150 · 250 · 400 ms
Busy · a shimmer on the acting card, never a spinner on the scene

Key moments: mask scan sweep (520 ms), zone settling from dashed to solid, and the lamp ignition, which has two dips in 340 ms, stays under three flashes a second, and only happens once.
