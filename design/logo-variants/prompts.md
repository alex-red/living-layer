## 01-layered-room

Use case: logo-brand. Create a beautiful minimal clean standalone icon for Living Layer, a local-first app that gives everyday objects memory through a camera and an isometric room twin. Premium, precise, quiet, warm technology. Square canvas. One centered large symbol with ample padding, flat vector-style geometry, crisp edges, consistent optical weight, instantly legible at 16–32 px. Transparent background. No text, lettering, labels, watermark, mockup, shadows, glow, gradients, texture, perspective presentation, or extra icons. Existing palette: cyan #6fd3e0, soft pink #e68cc4, ink #11141d. Use at most two colors. Design direction: a compact abstract isometric room corner built from two nested open angular layers. The silhouette should evoke a room and two layers of awareness, with a generous negative-space center. Mostly cyan with one restrained pink lower edge. Distinctive, balanced, extremely simple; maximum 3 structural strokes. It must feel like a considered brand symbol, not a detailed architectural illustration.
